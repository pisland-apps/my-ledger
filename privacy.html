My Ledger – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

My Ledger is a personal money-tracking web app. It runs entirely in your own browser and has no server of its own. This page explains what data the app handles and where that data goes.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (transactions, budgets, accounts, categories, notes, and attached photos or PDFs) is stored on your own device, in your browser's IndexedDB. If you set an app passcode, your records are encrypted on the device with AES-GCM. The developer runs no server and cannot see, access or recover your data.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This lets the app create and read its own files in a hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read, change or delete any of your other Drive files.

  • ledger-backup.json and ledger-img-<id>.json (your data and your attachments) are encrypted on your device with your passcode before upload. Google stores only the encrypted files.
  • ledger-primary.json is a small file that is not encrypted. It records which of your devices may upload automatically: a random device identifier, a device name, a mode and a timestamp. It contains none of your financial data.
  • Files are transferred directly between your browser and Google. They never pass through any server run by the developer.
  • The app reads the email address of the Google account you signed in with (through the Drive API) only to show which account is connected and to avoid mixing up accounts. It is kept on your device and is not sent anywhere else.
  • The Google access token is kept in your browser's memory and is not stored.

Exchange rates (optional)

Only when you tap the button to fetch the latest exchange rates, the app asks the public service open.er-api.com for rates. The request contains only a currency code (for example, your base currency). No personal or financial data is sent. As with any website, that service can see your IP address.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of your data to anyone.
  • No use of Google user data for anything other than syncing your own backup between your own devices.

My Ledger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Local data: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app from your home screen.
  • Drive data: disconnect Google Drive in the app, then open your Google Account's third-party access page, select My Ledger, and choose to delete its hidden app data. You can also revoke access there.
